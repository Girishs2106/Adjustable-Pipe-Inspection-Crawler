# Adjustable Pipe Inspection & Cleaning Crawler
**Mechanical Design | CAD Modelling | Mechanism Development | Ongoing Project**

## 1. Project Overview

The Adjustable Pipe Inspection & Cleaning Crawler is an ongoing mechanical engineering project focused on developing a compact crawler that can travel inside pipelines with internal diameters ranging from **6 to 12 inches (150–300 mm)**.

The proposed system combines an adjustable wheel-arm mechanism, motorized mobility, camera-based inspection, sensors, and a high-pressure water-cleaning arrangement.

The aim is to develop a crawler that can inspect internal pipe conditions and assist in removing deposits and blockages.

## 2. Problem Statement

Pipeline inspection and cleaning can be difficult when access is limited, particularly in narrow pipelines, bends, and areas affected by internal deposits.

Conventional manual inspection may require dismantling sections of a pipeline or using specialized equipment.

This project explores a compact, adjustable crawler that can move inside different pipe diameters while carrying inspection and cleaning components.

## 3. Objectives

- Design a compact crawler for 6–12 inch pipelines.
- Develop an adjustable wheel-arm mechanism to accommodate different pipe diameters.
- Provide motorized movement inside horizontal and vertical pipe sections.
- Integrate a camera and lighting system for internal inspection.
- Explore sensor-based distance detection and obstacle awareness.
- Develop a high-pressure water-cleaning arrangement.
- Maintain a compact mechanical layout with accessible components.
- Evaluate the design through calculations, fabrication, and testing.

## 4. Mechanical Design

The proposed crawler consists of a central chassis connected to adjustable radial support arms.

The main mechanical design includes:

- Central structural body.
- Adjustable support arms.
- Wheel brackets and drive wheels.
- Geared DC motor arrangement.
- Camera and lighting mounting.
- Water-line routing and nozzle arrangement.
- Component mounting and protective structure.

The design focuses on balancing compactness, stability, traction, and accessibility for maintenance.

## 5. Adjustable Wheel Mechanism

An adjustable wheel-arm mechanism is being developed to accommodate variations in internal pipe diameter.

The mechanism is intended to:

- Maintain contact between the wheels and the pipe's inner surface.
- Keep the crawler approximately centred.
- Provide sufficient traction during movement.
- Support movement in different pipe orientations.
- Allow adjustment for different pipe diameters.

A mechanically actuated adjustment arrangement, such as a rack-and-pinion system, is being considered for controlling the wheel-arm position.
